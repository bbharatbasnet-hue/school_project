About Junior Cambridge Secondary School

Our Story

Two Decades of Educating Nepalgunj

Founded in 2001 by a small group of educators who believed Nepalgunj deserved a school that combined academic rigour with genuine care, Junior Cambridge Secondary School began with just 60 students and four classrooms.

Today, we serve over 1,240 students across ECD through Grade 10, with a campus that includes modern science labs, a library, sports grounds, and dedicated arts studios — while holding onto the same close-knit, values-driven culture we started with.

Our Mission

To deliver holistic, student-centred education that builds strong academic foundations alongside character, creativity and civic responsibility.

Our Vision

To be Lumbini Province's most trusted school — recognised for nurturing confident, capable, and compassionate global citizens.

Our Objectives

Raise academic standards year on year, expand co-curricular access, and maintain a safe, inclusive campus for every learner.

Principal's Message

"Every Child Deserves to Be Truly Known"

Education today asks more of our children than ever before — not just to memorise, but to think critically, collaborate, and adapt. At Junior Cambridge, our teachers are trained to see each student as an individual, not a number.

We measure success not only in exam results, but in the confidence, kindness, and curiosity our graduates carry with them. I look forward to welcoming your family into ours.

— Mr. Rajendra Thapa, Principal (M.Ed.)

Discipline & Conduct

School Rules

  • Full school uniform is mandatory on all working days.
  • Students must arrive by 9:45 AM; gates close at 10:00 AM sharp.
  • Mobile phones must remain switched off during school hours.
  • Respect for staff, peers, and campus property is non-negotiable.
  • Three unexcused absences require a parent meeting.
