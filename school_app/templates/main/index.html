About Our School

A Complete Learning Ecosystem

Founded in 2001, Junior Cambridge Secondary School has grown into one of Nepalgunj's most trusted institutions — blending the national curriculum with Cambridge-style teaching methods.

Modern Curriculum

Blended national & Cambridge-aligned syllabus with project-based learning.

Science & Tech Labs

Fully-equipped physics, chemistry, biology and computer laboratories.

Sports & Arts

Football grounds, basketball courts, music & art studios for every child.

Safe Campus

CCTV-monitored campus with trained counsellors and a nurse on site.
