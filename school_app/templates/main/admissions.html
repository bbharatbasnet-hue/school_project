How It Works

Admission Procedure

1. Submit Application

Fill the online or paper application form with required documents.

2. Assessment

A short grade-appropriate written & oral assessment is scheduled.

3. Interview

A brief meeting with the student and parents/guardians.

4. Confirmation

Offer letter issued; seat confirmed upon fee payment.

Requirements

Eligibility

  • ECD: Age 3+ as of April 2026
  • Grade 1: Age 5+, basic readiness assessment
  • Grade 2–9: Report card from previous school required
  • Grade 10: Subject to seat availability & interview
Checklist

Required Documents

Common Questions

Admissions FAQs

Is there an entrance exam for all grades?

A short readiness assessment applies from Grade 2 onward; ECD and Grade 1 admissions are interview-based only.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes — merit-based and sibling-discount scholarships are available; ask the admissions office for details.

Is school transport available?

Yes, we operate bus routes covering most areas of Nepalgunj at an additional monthly fee.

When does the 2026 session begin?

Classes begin the first week of Baishakh (mid-April); orientation is one week prior.
